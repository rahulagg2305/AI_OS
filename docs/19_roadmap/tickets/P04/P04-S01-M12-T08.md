**Goal:** Expose comparison results for consumption.
**Input:** Computed comparisons.
**Output:** A queryable report.

`EvaluationReportingInterface.get_report()` composes the real
`SqlComparisonComputer` (no parallel mechanism) and returns a
`ComparisonReport` — a pure, synchronous wrapper over the already-
computed `ExperimentComparison`, queryable by variant (`get_variant`),
by variant-and-metric (`get_metric`), and by metric across every
variant at once (`compare_by_metric` — the one genuinely new,
cross-variant view; a lookup-only wrapper would not satisfy §4's own
"Comparison Report" definition). Proved end to end against real
Postgres. No persisted Results Store — recomputed live from
already-persisted data on every call, honestly disclosed rather than
adding an unapproved schema change.
