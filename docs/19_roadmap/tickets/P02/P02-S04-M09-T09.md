**Goal:** §5's Version Manager — decide what a new index generation means.
**Input:** Re-indexed or superseded content.
**Output:** A managed `index_generation`, and a real re-index trigger.
Verified 2026-08-13: `P02-S04-M09-T05` made version provenance real for
*reporting* — every result carries `chunk_strategy_version` and, when a
matching embedding exists, `embedding_model_id`/`_version`/
`index_generation`. `knowledge_manager.md` is explicit that nothing
"decides or manages what creating a real second generation would mean,
or triggers a re-index; that remains a separate, larger, undecided gap".
**Expect a design fork, and expect it to be the largest in this module.**
What forces a new generation (a chunker change, an embedding-model
change, a content change), whether old generations are retained or
archived, and what a query means when generations disagree are all
undecided and interact. Do this last in module 9, and scope it with the
product owner before writing code.
