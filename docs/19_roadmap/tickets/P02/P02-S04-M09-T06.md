**Goal:** Make freshly-indexed content genuinely vector-searchable.
**Input:** Content passing through `IndexingService`.
**Output:** Real embeddings written alongside the real chunks.
`indexing.py` contained no call to `embed` at all; it now calls the real, unchanged `embed_chunk` once per freshly-written chunk.
**The flagged fork was genuine and was escalated.** Product owner chose
opt-in in-line: `embedder`/`embedding_writer`/`embedding_model_alias`,
all three together or none (a partial set raises — silently indexing
without vectors is the exact gap this closes). No args = byte-identical.
**Disclosed, not discovered later:** chunks and vectors commit in
sequential transactions, not one — ADR-0013's intent, not its letter,
since `SqlEmbeddingWriter` owns its own and holding one open across N
billable calls is what this codebase avoids. A mid-way failure leaves a
partly-embedded document and propagates loudly; re-index is recovery.
8 real tests: a real `SqlVectorSearcher` finds the just-indexed chunks,
and a request-counting server proves an unchanged re-index costs zero.
