**Goal:** Make freshly-indexed content genuinely vector-searchable.
**Input:** Content passing through `IndexingService`.
**Output:** Real embeddings written alongside the real chunks.
Verified 2026-08-13: `knowledge_manager/indexing.py` contains no call to
`embed` at all. `knowledge_manager.md` states it plainly — `IndexingService`
"produces no embeddings, so a real caller wanting vector search over
freshly-indexed content must still compute and write that separately;
nothing does yet".
Both prerequisites are real and proven against Postgres/pgvector:
`retrieval.embedding_writer.SqlEmbeddingWriter`/`embed_chunk` and
`retrieval.vector_search.SqlVectorSearcher`. This connects them.
**Expect a design fork:** embedding during indexing makes indexing slow
and billable (it calls the real LLM Gateway `embed()`), so synchronous-
in-line vs a separate pass over unembedded chunks is a real choice with
real cost consequences. Stop and ask.
