**Goal:** `caching_strategy.md` §1's own `Cache` Protocol.
**Input:** A cache operation.
**Output:** A Protocol plus a real in-memory implementation.
`ResponseCache` was bound directly to a real `Redis`, so exercising it
needed a container — a large part of why nothing outside this package
ever touched caching. It now takes a `Cache`; `RedisCache` is the
production adapter, `InMemoryCache` the real fake (ADR-0004).
**`get`/`set`/`delete` are grounded, not invented**: the document
declines to fix a signature, so read/write come from what `ResponseCache`
genuinely does against Redis and `delete` from §2's own explicit
"support explicit invalidation" requirement.
**An adapter, not structural typing** — mypy confirmed `Redis` does not
satisfy the Protocol (`name`/`ex` vs `key`/`ttl_seconds`). TTL honoured
on a monotonic clock. 10 tests, incl. a real `ResponseCache` round trip
with no Redis at all; ADR-0025's exclusion still structural.
