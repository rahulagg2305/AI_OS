**Goal:** Make `evaluation.gate_results.duration_ms` a real measurement.
**Input:** A gate execution.
**Output:** The genuine wall-clock duration of that gate.
Verified 2026-08-13: `evaluation_engine.md` records that `duration_ms`
is "honestly `0` today", because both real write paths that feed
`SqlGateResultRecorder` stamp `workflow_steps.started_at` and
`completed_at` identically. The column is real, populated, and
meaningless — an honest constant, disclosed rather than fabricated,
but still a hole in §9's own execution-recording requirement.
Small and well-defined: the fix is to measure the gate call itself
rather than derive a duration from two timestamps that are written
together.
Pairs naturally with `T10` — both are about gate execution being
observable — but is independent of it and can be executed either order.
No design fork expected.
