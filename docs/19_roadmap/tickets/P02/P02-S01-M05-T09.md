**Goal:** Execute a declared decision step instead of completing as a no-op.
**Input:** A decision step.
**Output:** A real branch outcome.

No field-level contract for decision-step branching existed in any document
or the flat, positional `steps` list — a genuine structural blocker, not a
gap. Product owner approved a minimal, closed-vocabulary contract
(`condition`: sourceStepId/field/equals; `branches`: {"true"/"false": stepId})
as part of this step. `DecisionStepExecutor` evaluates it for real against a
named prior step's own persisted output; `_resolve_next_step` reads the
decision's own persisted `branch` back to resolve the genuine next step —
proven jumping over a positionally-adjacent step to a non-adjacent real
target. Unwired into any real composition yet, mirroring `quality_gate`'s
own precedent.
