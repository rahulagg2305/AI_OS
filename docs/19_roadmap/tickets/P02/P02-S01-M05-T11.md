**Goal:** Invoke a child workflow and join on its result.
**Input:** A sub_workflow step.
**Output:** Child outputs in the parent.

Genuinely bigger than decision/parallel (T09/T10): requires a real, separate
`WorkflowInstance`. `WorkflowDefinitionCatalog` is write-only (no reader) —
stopped, presented 3 options, product owner approved composition-level
injection (the `gate_sources`/`step_retry_targets` shape) over a new catalog
reader. `SubWorkflowStepExecutor` creates/starts/runs a child instance via
its own injected `WorkflowInstanceService`/`WorkflowAdvanceRunner`, joins on
the child's own last-executed step output (`outputs` is never written by
`advance_workflow`). Proven by a real Postgres-backed integration test
(genuine child creation/completion, refused unconfigured `subWorkflowId`)
plus a fake-based unit test. Unwired into any real composition, mirroring
quality_gate/decision/parallel precedent.
