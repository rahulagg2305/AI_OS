**Goal:** Stop a permanently-failing step retrying forever, and persist the terminal state (R-016).
**Input:** A step failure on the worker loop.
**Output:** A bounded retry, then a persisted `failed` instance.

`run_once` had no bound: the lease released, status stayed `running`,
the next poll retried — forever. `failed` was declared in the nine-state
list and never written by anything.

Two product-owner decisions, asked not assumed: a **platform default**
`RetryPolicy` (2 attempts / 60s, mirroring the one real declared policy)
for the 2 of 3 definitions declaring none; and exhaustion always writes
**`failed`**, leaving `failureHandling.onError` declared-but-unread as a
recorded gap, not invented escalation. Attempts/duration come from
`workflow_steps`, already populated by `record_failed_attempt` (no
migration). `mark_failed` mirrors `cancel`'s CAS. 4 Postgres tests.
