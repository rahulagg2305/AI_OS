**Goal:** Enforce ADR-0023's per-class approver grant on decide() (FR-013, §9).
**Input:** A principal attempting to decide a pending approval.
**Output:** Authorized decides; unauthorized refused, approval stays pending.

Not on the ready list; investigated like T04/T05. `permissions.py`'s flat
role->permission dict can't express ADR-0023's own per-class grant
(`approver:release` distinct from `approver:architecture`), so added a
small, standalone `is_authorized_to_decide_approval` (security_manager),
reusing `Principal` unchanged — not a parallel mechanism. New
`ApprovalService` sits in front of `ApprovalRepository.decide()`; refuses
before any write (approval stays pending, proven), requiring `admin` or
the exact `approver:<approval_class>` role (`approval_class` = the point
id, T04/T05's own reuse). Proven via real Postgres: the class-scoped
approver genuinely decides; a wrong-class or role-less principal is
refused and the row stays pending throughout both attempts.
