**Goal:** Enforce the principal term at agent/tool resolution, alongside the real pack-grant term.
**Input:** A triggering principal's real SecurityContext.permissions.
**Output:** Resolution narrows to the principal's own permissions, not only the pack's grant.

Structural gap found: resolution (worker-loop ticks) can run with no live
bearer token at all. Stopped and proposed two designs; product owner chose
persisting a permissions snapshot at trigger time over live-object-only
threading. Added nullable `workflow_instances.principal_permissions`
(migration 0031 — first attempt used a >32-char revision id and broke
`alembic_version`, fixed), threaded through `create_instance`/`advance()`/
`StepExecutor.execute()` (mirrors `workflow_id`'s own precedent) to
`resolve_agent`/`resolve_tool`. New `narrowing.over_permitted_permissions`
reuses `intersect_declared_permissions`. Proven: a pack grants llm:invoke+
sandbox:execute (sufficient for the pack term alone); a principal holding
only sandbox:execute is genuinely refused resolving that same agent.
