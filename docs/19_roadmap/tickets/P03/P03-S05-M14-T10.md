**Goal:** Enforce a workflow's own declared permissions (ADR-0023) at agent/tool resolution.
**Input:** The resolving step's real workflow definition + its manifest-declared `permissions`.
**Output:** Resolution narrows to the workflow's own ceiling, not only pack-grant/principal terms.

Structural fork found (per ticket): where to derive `workflows[].permissions` from the
manifest. Chose pack-registration time (`derive_workflow_definition_rows`, mirroring
agent/tool rows) over threading a manifest lookup through `create_instance` (no real
caller holds one). `catalog.workflow_definitions.declared_permissions` (existing
column, never populated) now real; read by the already-stable `(definition_id,
version)` a `WorkflowInstance` pins — no snapshot needed, unlike the principal term.
`over_permitted_permissions` generalized (`bounding_permissions`) for both terms,
threaded `service.advance()` -> `StepExecutor.execute()` -> `resolve_agent`/
`resolve_tool`. Proven: pack grants llm:invoke+sandbox:execute (sufficient for the
pack term alone); a workflow declaring only sandbox:execute is genuinely refused
resolving that agent (principal term unsupplied, isolating this term as the cause).
