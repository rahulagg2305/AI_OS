**Goal:** Resolve secrets from Vault (ADR-0024).
**Input:** A Vault reference.
**Output:** A resolved secret.

`VaultSecretProvider` resolves `secret://vault/<name>[#<version>]`
against a real Vault KV v2 mount (token auth). Also built the real
`AIOS_SECRET_BACKEND` switch secrets_management.md §5 documented but
never implemented, and made the two hardcoded `secret://env/...`
references backend-aware (design fork, product-owner approved) so
setting the switch to `vault` genuinely moves them, not just new refs.
Proven against a real, self-hosted Vault dev-mode container.
