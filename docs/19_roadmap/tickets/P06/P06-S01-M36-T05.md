**Goal:** `POST /workflows/{id}/retry` — §6.1's last unbuilt documented route.
**Input:** A permanently `failed` instance.
**Output:** It runs again, from the step that failed.
`T17` closed R-016 by making `failed` real, and created a dead end: a workflow could fail for good with no operator recourse.
**The status flip is the easy half.** A `failed` instance has already
spent *both* retry bounds, so a bare flip grants one more attempt
regardless of the declared `retryPolicy` — `min(started_at)` is by then
far past `max_duration_seconds`. Product owner chose a **fresh** budget:
`retried_at` (migration `0039`) is a retry epoch, and
`step_failure_stats` counts only failures at or after it, read by
joining `workflow_instances`, so the Protocol signature is unchanged and
no fake can skip it. Straight to `running`, not the unreached
`waiting_for_retry`. Guarded CAS like `cancel`; 404 and 409 kept apart.
7 real Postgres tests through the real `build_app()`; the budget test
fails with "the step ran 1 time(s), not 2" when reverted.
